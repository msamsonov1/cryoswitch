Physical Mechanism of Asymmetric Thermal Runaway in 2G-HTS Tapes

High-temperature superconducting (HTS) fault current limiters based on rare-earth barium copper oxide ($\text{REBa}_2\text{Cu}_3\text{O}_{7-\delta}$, or REBCO) coated conductors operate at zero electrical resistance during nominal lunar microgrid power distribution. Under megawatt short-circuit events, the current exceeds the critical threshold ($I > I_c$). The core failure mode is not uniform heating, but asymmetric local quench driven by non-linear transition kinetics and cryogen vapor-film insulation.

1. Non-Linear E-J Power Law & Flux Flow Resistivity

When current density $J$ approaches critical current density $J_c(T, B)$, magnetic flux vortices depin from nanoscale pinning centers, generating electric fields governed by the non-linear power-law characteristic:

$$E(J, T) = E_c \left( \frac{J}{J_c(T)} \right)^{n(T)} + \rho_{\text{ff}} (J - J_c) \cdot \Theta(J - J_c)$$

Where $E_c = 1\,\mu\text{V/cm}$ is the critical electric field criterion and $n(T)$ represents the pinning creep exponent ($n \approx 25\text{--}35$ at $77\text{ K}$). Once temperature rises, $J_c(T)$ drops linearly according to $J_c(T) = J_{c0} \left(1 - \frac{T}{T_c}\right)^\alpha$, causing current to rapidly divert into the high-resistivity Hastelloy substrate ($\rho \approx 1.25\times 10^{-6}\,\Omega\cdot\text{m}$) and copper stabilizer jacket.

Critical Parameters: $T_c = 92.0\text{ K}$, $J_{c0} = 3.2\times 10^6\text{ A/cm}^2$ (self-field).
2. Cryogenic Heat Balance & Vapor Film Boiling Lock

The 1D longitudinal temperature distribution $T(x, t)$ across the superconductor tape is governed by the Fourier heat diffusion equation coupled to non-linear cryogen surface boiling heat flux $q_{\text{boil}}(T)$:

$$C_v(T) \frac{\partial T}{\partial t} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x} \left( k(T) \frac{\partial T}{\partial x} \right) + \frac{I^2(t) R_{\text{tape}}(T, x)}{A_{\text{cross}}} - \frac{P_{\text{wet}}}{A_{\text{cross}}} q_{\text{boil}}(T - T_{\text{bath}})$$

In subcooled liquid nitrogen, when $\Delta T = T - T_{\text{bath}} > 12\text{ K}$, heat flux hits the Critical Heat Flux peak ($q_{\text{CHF}} \approx 20\text{--}25\text{ W/cm}^2$) and drops precipitously into the film boiling regime. A blanket of nitrogen gas vapor thermally isolates the tape spot, reducing heat removal by an order of magnitude and initiating irreversible thermal runaway ($\frac{\partial^2 T}{\partial t^2} > 0$).

Thermodynamic Limits: $q_{\text{film}} \approx 2.5\text{ W/cm}^2 \ll q_{\text{CHF}}$.
3. Multiplexed FBG & Voltage-Tap State Observer

CryoSwitch continuously tracks localized temperature via Fiber Bragg Grating (FBG) optical wavelength shifts and segment voltage drop taps $V_k(t)$:

$$\Delta \lambda_B(x, t) = \lambda_{B0} \left( (\alpha_\Lambda + \xi) \Delta T(x, t) + (1 - p_e) \varepsilon_{\text{thermal}}(x, t) \right)$$

Coupled with segmental voltages $R_k(t) = \frac{V_k(t)}{I(t)}$, an extended Kalman observer continuously estimates the $(T, R)$ trajectory vector $\mathbf{x}_k = [T_k, \dot{T}_k, R_k, \dot{R}_k]^T$. An anomalous quench trip condition is triggered when:

$$\Psi(x) = \left( \frac{\partial R_k}{\partial T_k} \right) \cdot \left( \frac{\partial T_k}{\partial t} \right) > \Gamma_{\text{trip}} \quad \text{where } \Gamma_{\text{trip}} = 450\,\frac{\Omega\cdot\text{K}}{\text{s}}$$
Optical Polling Rate: 250 kHz via tunable Fabry-Pérot laser interrogator.
4. Fast Solid-State Current Bypass (SSCB)

Once $\Psi(x) > \Gamma_{\text{trip}}$, gate drive pulses actuate a bidirectional Silicon Carbide (SiC) / GaN JFET solid-state switch in parallel with the SFCL tape within $< 2\,\mu\text{s}$, commutating fault current into a water-cooled/cryogenic ballast resistor $R_{\text{shunt}}$:

$$I_{\text{tape}}(t) = I_{\text{fault}}(0) \cdot \exp\left( -\frac{R_{\text{SSCB}} + R_{\text{shunt}}}{L_{\text{grid}}} t \right)$$

Diverting current arrests the adiabatic Joule energy deposit $\int_0^{\tau} I^2(t) R(t) dt$ below $12\text{ J/cm}$, ensuring the peak hot-spot temperature never exceeds $135\text{ K}$, well below the critical delamination and debonding threshold ($T_{\text{destruct}} \approx 320\text{ K}$) of multi-layer REBCO architecture.

Thermal Runaway Protection: Margin of safety $> 185\text{ K}$.
